Mobile Computing Research & Evaluation Indicators

Telemetry
📶 Connectivity Simulator

Simulates WiFi, 4G, Weak 2G/3G Cellular, and Offline airplane mode with local cache fallback and queue synchronization.

🎯 GPS Accuracy Jitter (±3m to ±50m)

Models indoor multipath interference, urban canyon degradation, and dual-frequency GPS L1/L5 with visual uncertainty radius.

⚡ Alert Latency Instrumentation

Measures transmission propagation delay from Admin EOC dispatch to student device reception in milliseconds.

🔋 Battery Consumption Estimator

Calculates energy drain rate (%/hour) based on GPS update interval (1s vs 10s vs 60s) and cellular transmitter power level.

📦 Bandwidth Consumption Monitor

Tracks cumulative payload bytes sent and received during real-time tracking and SSE broadcast delivery.

👥 Multi-User Fleet Scalability

Simulates concurrent active campus students, displaying real-time location pings and hazard zone overlap on the Admin dashboard.
